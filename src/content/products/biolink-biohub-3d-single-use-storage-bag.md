## Overview

The **BioHub® 3D Single-Use Storage Bag** is a three-dimensional single-use bioprocess bag designed for liquid storage and transfer in biopharmaceutical processes. It is designed to fit BioLink plastic and stainless-steel containers.

BioLink states that it standardized bag dimensions to match commonly used 3D containers from different manufacturers, addressing inconsistencies in bag dimensions and supporting faster delivery. The product range is stated as 50 L to 3,000 L. Silicone tubing and thermoplastic tubing options are available for different liquid-transfer and storage requirements.

The bags are made from multilayer co-extrusion film, with low gas and water-vapor permeability, chemical compatibility, biocompatibility and heat-seal strength intended to support storage and transportation of process liquids.

## Key Features

- **Capacity range:** 50–3,000 L stated for the product family.
- **Standard bag specifications:** Published standard configurations are listed in the 50–500 L range.
- **Container compatibility:** Designed for BioLink plastic and stainless-steel containers, with configurations matched to common 3D containers.
- **Tubing options:** Platinum-cured silicone tubing and thermoplastic tubing.
- **Multilayer co-extrusion film:** Designed for low gas and water-vapor permeability, chemical compatibility and biocompatibility.
- **HDPE container material:** The 3D containers are made from high-density polyethylene (HDPE).
- **Flared design:** Facilitates flanging and fixing the bag liner.
- **Smooth internal surfaces:** Clean, uniform inner walls are intended to help prevent damage to the flexible bag.
- **Operating temperature:** −20 °C to 60 °C.
- **Autoclaving:** Not allowed.

## Applications

The product is intended for storage and transportation of feed liquids and other process liquids in biopharmaceutical manufacturing.

Potential uses should be selected according to the liquid, container type, tubing assembly and operating conditions. Confirm chemical compatibility and suitability with BioLink for the intended process.
