## Overview

The **BioHub® 2D Single-Use Storage Bag** is a disposable 2D biocontainer designed for bioprocess liquid handling. BioLink describes it as suitable for liquid storage, mixing, transport and cryopreservation.

The bag is manufactured from multi-layer co-extruded film. Its integrated, ship-shaped welded outlet is designed to minimize residual liquid. Outlet sizes are available in 1/8", 1/4" and 3/8", and can be connected to silicone or thermoplastic tubing. Assemblies can be configured with different connectors, hoses and functional components.

## Key Features

- **Volume range:** 5 mL–50 L.
- **Multi-layer co-extruded film** for biopharmaceutical applications.
- **Integrated welded outlet** with a ship-shaped design to help minimize residual liquid.
- **Outlet sizes:** 1/8", 1/4" and 3/8".
- **Tubing compatibility:** Silicone and thermoplastic tubing.
- **Configurable assemblies:** Different connectors, hoses and functional units.
- **Applications:** Collection of purified components, bulk solution storage, intermediate product storage and media storage.

## Applications

BioLink lists the following applications:

- Collection of purified components.
- Bulk solution and buffer storage.
- Culture-medium storage.
- Harvest of raw liquid and sample collection.
- Preservation of intermediate products.
- Transportation of final products.
- Liquid transport and cryopreservation.

## BioHub® Film

BioLink identifies Renolit as the film supplier for its BioHub® 2D Single-Use Storage Bags. The film is described as a biopharmaceutical-dedicated, multi-layer co-extruded material. BioLink states that material-quality certificates and validation documents are available.
