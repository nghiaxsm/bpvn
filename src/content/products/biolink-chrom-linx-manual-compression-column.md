## Product Overview

The Chrom-LinX® Manual Compression Chromatography Column is designed for
purification applications and chromatography process development in
biopharmaceuticals. The columns can be packed with commonly used
chromatography media, including soft gels such as dextran-based agarose,
and hard gels such as poly(styrene divinylbenzene) (PS-DVB) and
hydroxyapatite.

The platform uses an industry-adopted rotation compression sealing
method. Its simplified design is intended to improve user experience and
the efficiency and success of media packing. Optional accessories and
spare parts are available, including tubing, valves and consumables.

## Key Features

-   **SCHOTT borosilicate glass column tubing:** Precise inner diameter
    and a smooth coaxial circumference support separation and
    purification.
-   **Column sieves:** Upper and lower sieves use USP \<88\> Class
    material and BioLink's customized plastic sealing process, providing
    low flow pressure difference, high liquid dispersion ability and
    distribution performance.
-   **Sealing components:** EPDM components in accordance with USP
    \<88\> Class provide sealing performance with minimal dead-cavity
    volume.
-   **Pharmaceutical-grade wetted metal parts:** Fluid-contact metal
    parts are made from stainless steel for rigidity and corrosion
    resistance.
-   **Sanitary connections:** Sanitary Tri-Clamp quick fittings and a
    sanitary flow-path design support column cleanliness.
-   **Manual operation:** Screw-lifting and O-ring sealing handles are
    designed for easier operation.
-   **Drainage design:** A side outlet interface and micro-slope bottom
    are designed to support liquid drainage and cleaning.
-   **Packing reservoirs:** Adaptive column packing reservoirs are
    available in multiple specifications for higher column beds.
