## Product Overview

The **CytoLinX® GB 1–20 L Benchtop Glass Bioreactor** is a configurable glass bioreactor platform available in 1 L, 3 L, 7 L, 15 L and 20 L vessel sizes. The system consists of a glass vessel and a control cabinet and is designed for upstream process development of biologics.

The platform supports process parameter exploration and culture-process optimization. It is applicable to different cell types, including suspension cells, adherent cells, plant cells and insect cells. The design allows the use of existing brands of electrodes, tubing, gas distributors and stirring paddles.

The vessel uses top-mounted stirring. Large-bubble and microbubble gas distribution options can be configured to support different culture-process requirements.

## Key Features

### Flexible vessel configuration

- Available in 1 L, 3 L, 7 L, 15 L and 20 L total vessel volumes.
- Single-wall and jacketed vessel options are listed for selected vessel sizes.
- Configurable ports and accessories support different process requirements.
- The platform allows use of existing brands of electrodes, tubes, gas distributors and stirring paddles.

### Stirring and gas distribution

- Top-mounted stirring system driven by a servo brushless motor.
- Stirring speed range: 0–1,500 rpm.
- Large-bubble and microbubble gas distribution options.
- Gas distribution options include L-type macrobubble, annular macrobubble and microbubble distributors.
- Overlay and sparge ventilation modes.

### Control and automation

- Control cabinet built on the Siemens WinCC framework.
- Industrial PLC controller.
- 23-inch PC interface.
- Supports local data storage, batch control, recipe management, third-party data uploads, audit tracking and PAT data control.
- The manufacturer states that the control system design complies with 21 CFR Part 11 requirements.
- A single control cabinet can interface with different models of BioLink glass vessels.

### Process monitoring and PAT options

- pH and dissolved oxygen (DO) sensor options.
- Pt100 temperature sensor.
- Optional level/antifoam control.
- Optional pCO₂, cell-density, conductance and Raman electrodes.
- PAT tools can be configured according to process and application requirements.

### Temperature control

- Electric heating blanket for single-wall vessels.
- TCU heating for jacketed vessels.

## Applications

The manufacturer identifies the CytoLinX® GB platform for the following applications:

### Upstream cell culture and process development

- Suspension-cell culture for antibody, gene therapy and cell therapy applications.
- Adherent-cell suspension culture on microcarriers for vaccine development.
- Seed amplification and microorganism alkaline lysis in plasmid production for mRNA therapy and vaccines.
- Amplification steps in stem-cell and T-cell cultivation processes.

### ADC conjugation process optimization

BioLink describes an automated platform for ADC conjugation process optimization. The platform uses precise temperature control and programmable automatic dosing to support repeatability and stability of the reaction system. The vessel design is described as suitable for manufacturing and linear scale-up. Online pH sensors can be configured for process-development monitoring and control.

### mRNA production

BioLink describes use of the CytoLinX® GB platform for in-vitro transcription (IVT) process development and mRNA production. The automated platform combines precise temperature control and programmable automatic dosing to support repeatability and linear scale-up from laboratory development to larger-scale production.
