## Product Overview

The **PuriLinX® Benchtop Chromatography System** is a laboratory-scale chromatography platform developed by BioLink for chromatography process development. The brochure describes the system as featuring accurate and stable flow delivery, high pressure resistance and sensitive detection.

Its software integrates instrument control, method editing, data analysis, standard-curve functions, report generation, user-permission management and audit trails. The brochure states that the software is designed to comply with relevant FDA, GLP and GMP regulations.

The PuriLinX® platform is available with different pump-flow configurations. Configurations can also vary by inlet channels, outlet channels, column-valve arrangement, UV detector and fraction collector.

## Key Features

### Configurable chromatography platform

- High-configuration system options include dual pumps, dual bubble sensors, dual-pump back-pressure valve, column valve, multi-channel loading valve, multi-channel outlet valve and DAD ultraviolet detector.
- Multiple pump-flow configurations are available to suit different chromatography process-development requirements.
- Configurations can be selected to suit different chromatography process-development requirements.

### Accurate and stable pump delivery

- Classic duplex-plunger parallel pump design.
- Advanced cam technology and a double-piston mechanism provide virtually pulse-free fluid delivery, according to the brochure.
- Small pulse-damper dead volume.
- Designed for chromatographic separation and liquid-phase analysis.
- Standard fluid-path materials are stainless steel and PEEK.

### Modular DAD detection

- DAD400 detector uses a modular design with an independent coupled light source and a sensitive polychromator spectral-analysis system.
- Czerny–Turner polychromator design.
- Fiber-optic connection to the flow cell helps isolate the heat source and facilitates maintenance.
- Multiple wavelengths can be set and monitored simultaneously.
- No mechanical moving parts in the optical detection system.

### Online bubble monitoring

- Ultrasonic bubble sensor for online fluid-bubble monitoring.
- Non-contact detection without ultrasonic couplants.
- Does not damage the sample.
- Dual-colour LED indicator.
- The brochure lists sensor A, sensor B and sensor S options.

### Flexible sample loading and fraction collection

- Multi-channel inlet configurations for pump A and pump B.
- Single-, three- or four-column valve configurations, depending on options.
- Two-, six- or eight-outlet valve configurations.
- Fraction collector options include open, closed and temperature-controlled designs.

### Instrument and software integration

- Instrument and software linkage control.
- Wireless connection is supported, according to the brochure.
- Software provides system control, method editing, result analysis and system management.
- Automatic data backup is described for data security.

## Applications

The manufacturer positions PuriLinX® for laboratory-scale chromatography and chromatography process development.

The brochure specifically describes software-supported analysis for:

- Small-scale screening
- Chromatography method development
- Process scale-up studies

The brochure does not provide a definitive list of target biomolecules or validated purification workflows. Confirm application suitability and configuration with BioLink for the intended process.

## Main System Modules

### Pump system

The system uses a duplex-plunger parallel pump with control and mechanical components designed for stable long-term operation and accurate flow output.

- Small dead volume of the pulse damper
- Advanced cam technology and double-piston mechanism
- Large flow range, high accuracy and low pulsation
- Standard fluid-path materials: stainless steel and PEEK

### DAD detector

The DAD400 is an ultraviolet diode-array detector described for chromatography, environmental protection and online monitoring.

- Independent coupled light source
- Sensitive polychromator spectral-analysis system
- Czerny–Turner optical design
- Fiber-optic flow-cell connection
- Simultaneous monitoring at multiple wavelengths
- Rich output interfaces

### Bubble sensor

The ultrasonic bubble sensor provides online monitoring of bubbles in the fluid path.

- Non-contact detection
- No ultrasonic couplant required
- No sample damage
- Dual-colour LED status indicator

### Collection rack

The collection rack can be used independently or with a protein purification system.

Supported collection formats listed in the brochure:

- 96-well plates
- 13 mm test-tube racks
- 16 mm test-tube racks
- 30 mm test-tube racks

The test-tube rack tray is designed to accommodate the different collection types without changing the tray. The collector also provides external interfaces and a separate touchscreen control system.

### Mixer and online sensors

- **Mixer:** Dynamic, electromagnetically driven mixer; mixing-pool volume 2 mL.
- **pH detector:** Includes temperature compensation; range 0–14 pH; precision 0.1 pH.
- **Conductivity detector:** Includes temperature compensation; range 0–999.999 mS/cm; precision ±2%.
