## Product Overview

The **B Pilot 750** is a benchtop chromatography system designed for product development, pilot-scale research, and small-scale GMP-grade production purification. It supports process development and technology transfer, and can be used for rapid proof-of-concept (PoC) studies.

The system combines a sanitary diaphragm pump, flow measurement, and an intelligent compression-column platform. Its compact panel design and patented pipe connectors are intended to simplify system setup and operation.

## Key Features

- **Multi-wavelength UV detection:** Standard deuterium-lamp-based variable-wavelength UV detector. Optional long-life single- or dual-wavelength UV detectors using LED light sources are available; the manufacturer states a service life of more than 8,000 hours for these LED options.
- **Constant-flow control:** Dual high-precision ultrasonic flow meters and PID control support constant-flow operation.
- **Sanitary diaphragm pump:** High-performance diaphragm pump designed to meet GMP requirements.
- **Flexible column configuration:** Supports single-column or double-column configurations, including two columns connected in series.
- **Intelligent column packing:** Works with the BSC series benchtop intelligent compression column and supports intelligent column packing.
- **Quick connection:** Patented hard-pipe connectors are designed to simplify connections.
- **Control software:** CBS control software provides system control and is stated by the manufacturer to comply with FDA 21 CFR Part 11 requirements.
- **Automation options:** DCS and batch automation platform software are available as options.

## Applications

- Product development and chromatography process development
- Pilot-scale purification research
- Small-scale GMP-grade production purification
- Proof-of-concept (PoC) studies
- Process scale-up preparation and technology transfer
