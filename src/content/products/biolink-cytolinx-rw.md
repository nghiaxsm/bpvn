## Product Overview

The **CytoLinX® RW** is a single-use rocking bioreactor platform from BioLink, available in 10 L, 20 L, 50 L, 100 L and 200 L system sizes. The product page describes working-volume ranges from 0.3–25 L for the 10–50 L systems and 10–100 L for the 100–200 L systems.

During operation, sterile-filtered air or oxygen is introduced into the culture bag. The bag is placed on a rocking platform, causing the culture medium to move in a wave-like motion. This provides oxygen supply and cell mixing while maintaining a mild culture environment with low shear force.

The system is intended for cell culture applications including scientific research, process development, in-process seed culture and new therapies such as cell therapy.

## Key Features

### Low-shear rocking culture

- Uses a rocking motion to mix the culture medium and support oxygen transfer.
- Designed to reduce cell damage associated with shear force and bubbling at impeller ends in traditional mixing vessels.
- Rocking speed and angle can be adjusted to suit process requirements.

### Flexible system and working-volume range

- System sizes include 10 L, 20 L, 50 L, 100 L and 200 L.
- The 10–50 L systems support working volumes from 0.3–25 L, depending on tray size.
- The 100–200 L systems support working volumes from 10–100 L.

### Process control and monitoring

- Siemens PLC control.
- PID feedback control and optical compensation for fiber-optic electrodes.
- Process data acquisition, analysis, comparison and report printing.
- Monitoring, alarm and protection functions for fault sensing, diagnosis and alerts.
- The manufacturer states that the software is compliant with 21 CFR Part 11 and supports audit trails.

### Flexible process configuration

- Optional ventilation, pH/DO and perfusion modules.
- Supports perfusion and automatic calibration of pump flow rate for feeding and harvesting.
- Optional nitrogen mass flow controller for DO-associated N₂ reverse regulation.
- Consumable options include basic, pH & DO electrode, and perfusion types.

### Adjustable operating parameters

- Variable-frequency speed regulation.
- Rocking speed range: 2–40 RPM.
- Rocking angle range: 2–14°.
- Electric heating for temperature control.
