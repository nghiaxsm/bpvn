## Product Overview

The **B Pilot CX750** is a pilot-scale purification platform integrating chromatography (CHROM) and tangential flow filtration (TFF) in a single system. According to the manufacturer, its optimized flow-path architecture and upgraded monitoring modules enable users to switch between chromatography and ultrafiltration without hardware modification or tubing reconnection.

The platform is designed to support process development, process scale-up and process validation for antibodies, vaccines, recombinant proteins, other biologics and cell and gene therapy (CGT) manufacturing.

## Key Features

### Dual-function chromatography and TFF

- One system supports both chromatography and TFF processes.
- Switching between functions does not require disassembly or tubing reconnection.

### Wide process compatibility

- Flow-rate range and membrane-area compatibility are intended to cover requirements from process development to pilot-scale production.
- Compatible with most chromatography columns and TFF cassettes.
- Applications include purification of monoclonal antibodies (mAbs), vaccines, recombinant proteins and gene therapy products.

### Reduced footprint and capital investment

- Integrates chromatography and ultrafiltration functions into one platform.
- Designed to reduce cleanroom footprint and capital expenditure compared with using separate systems.

### Flexible and scalable operation

- The automated platform acquires pilot-scale process parameters for key process steps to support process transfer.
- System architecture is designed to accommodate future process upgrades and scale-up requirements.

### GMP-oriented design

- Product-contact components are stated to meet USP Class VI requirements.
- Supports electronic signatures, audit trails and complete data records in accordance with 21 CFR Part 11, as stated in the brochure.
- Supports clean-in-place (CIP) for cleaning and changeover between batches and products.

## Applications

- Pilot-scale chromatography purification
- Tangential flow filtration (TFF)
- Process development and optimization
- Process scale-up and process validation
- Purification of monoclonal antibodies, vaccines and recombinant proteins
- Purification processes for gene therapy products and other biologics
- CGT manufacturing workflows

## Process Flow and System Configuration

The brochure provides separate process flow diagrams for the chromatography and TFF operating modes:

- **Chromatography flow diagram (page 4):** shows the A and B inlet valve blocks, dual pumps and flow meters, column flow path, monitoring points and six product outlet positions (P1–P6).
- **TFF flow diagram (page 5):** shows the recirculation loop and TFF module arrangement, alongside the system's inlet, pump, pressure and monitoring components.

The diagrams illustrate the two process configurations supported by the integrated platform. Refer to the manufacturer's system documentation for detailed operating procedures, tubing configuration and process-specific setup.

## Software and Data Management

The control software is developed using LabWindows/CVI. The brochure states that the software is designed to comply with FDA 21 CFR Part 11 requirements. DeltaV is listed as an optional platform.

Software functions described by the manufacturer include:

- **Electronic records and signatures:** access control and password management.
- **Recipe editing:** configuration of flow rate, valve switching, gradient modes (CV, L or min) and other operating parameters.
- **Audit trail:** records system operations and supports PDF report generation.
- **Data backup and recovery:** automatic and manual backup functions.
- **System logs:** records operating information to support process and system diagnostics.
- **Alarm and warning management:** configurable alarms and recovery logic.
- **Database-based data management:** stores data in a database for stability and searchability.
