## Overview

The **BioHub® Sealer Ⅱ** is designed for the sterile disconnection of disposable components used in biopharmaceutical manufacturing processes. It is intended to maintain product sterility during disconnection, including when the operation is performed in a non-sterile environment.

The BioHub® series includes aseptic tube welders, sealers and disconnecting tools for aseptic connection and disconnection operations in single-use bioprocessing systems. The Sealer Ⅱ is compatible with single-use bags, tubing assemblies and process components.

## Key Features

### Wide tubing size range

- Applicable to tubing sizes from **OD 1/4″ to OD 1″**.
- Covers commonly used tubing diameters in the biopharmaceutical industry.

### Tubing material compatibility

- Suitable for **C-Flex™**, **AdvantaFlex™** and **B-Flex™** tubing.
- Sealing processes for tubing from other brands can be customized.

### Flexible operation

- Supports operation with tubing lengths as short as **17 cm**.
- Can be hung vertically or placed horizontally, depending on the operating environment.
- The display screen can rotate for use in either orientation.

### Fast sealing and cooling

- Heat-sealing process takes approximately **3–4.5 minutes**, depending on tubing size and TPE material.
- Provides two cooling modes: high-speed and medium-speed.
- Cooling method: air cooling.

### Portable design

- Net weight: **4 kg**.
- Compact dimensions: **390 × 157 × 142 mm**.

### Touchscreen operation and process guidance

- Equipped with a **5-inch touchscreen**.
- Displays the sealing process in real time.
- Provides step-by-step operator guidance and process monitoring.

### Sealing performance

- Supports sealing of:
  - Empty tubing.
  - Tubing containing liquid.
  - Tubing containing liquid under pressurized conditions.
- Seal width: up to **20 mm**.
- Includes a visible cutting guide line to support accurate disconnection.

### Data management and equipment stability

- Five permission levels are available.
- Provides audit-trail functionality and function classification.
- Includes a built-in aging-test program.
- Each unit undergoes up to **20 sealing test cycles** before leaving the factory, according to BioLink.

## Applications

The product is intended for aseptic disconnection operations in single-use biopharmaceutical manufacturing systems. Typical system components identified by BioLink include:

- Single-use bags.
- Tubing assemblies.
- Disposable process components.

The manufacturer describes the equipment as suitable for maintaining sterile product handling during disconnection in non-sterile environments.
