## Product Overview

The Chrom-LinX® Automated Chromatography Column is designed for
downstream purification of biopharmaceuticals. It is operated by
automated control software through the BioLink Packing-LinX® column
packing workstation, reducing the manual operations associated with
conventional chromatography columns.

When used with the BioLink Slurry-LinX® series slurry tank, the column
packing process can remain in a closed environment for most of the
operation, helping minimize the risk of exposure and contamination of
the chromatography resin and homogenizing solution.

The column is designed for low-pressure operation and axial compression.
The product overview describes standard inner diameters of 450, 600,
800, 1000 and 1200 mm, with a column tube height of 700 mm and an
optional customized height of 1000 mm. It is intended for manufacturing
bioproducts at different types and scales.

The platform is compatible with BioLink ChromaX® chromatography resins
and is also described as suitable for Chromstar FF and MaXtar series
resins with high rigidity and flow rate. The Packing-LinX® workstation
includes preset packing methods for relevant resins.

## Key Features

-   **Automated column operation:** The column is controlled by software
    through the Packing-LinX® workstation, reducing conventional manual
    operation.
-   **Automated packing and maintenance:** Programmed operations include
    packing, dismantling, loading, unloading and maintenance.
-   **Servo-driven column head:** The column head moves vertically under
    servo-motor control. Its movement speed can be set through software,
    with a stated movement-distance accuracy of 1 mm.
-   **Controlled resin bed formation:** The column head compresses the
    pre-homogenized resin to form a uniform and stable bed structure.
    Bed height can be controlled through the workstation.
-   **Visible column bed:** The acrylic column tube provides a clear
    view of the column bed position.
-   **Sanitary design:** The product is described as having a sanitary
    design in line with GMP requirements and a structure intended to
    avoid cleaning dead corners.
-   **Designed for multiple packing applications:** The column is stated
    to support a variety of packing methods.
-   **Optional sieve aperture:** Sieve plate apertures of 10 μm and 20
    μm are available.
-   **Closed packing process:** When used with the Slurry-LinX® series
    slurry tank, most of the packing process can be conducted in a
    closed environment.

## Packing-LinX® Column Packing Workstation

The Packing-LinX® Column Packing Workstation controls operation of the
Chrom-LinX® Automated Chromatography Column through a touchscreen
interface.

The workstation supports venting, filling, disassembly and maintenance
functions, as well as corresponding programs that operators can select
and configure. During packing, the equipment stops automatically when
the preset compression factor or column bed height is reached. The
operator can also stop the process manually at any time.

The product page describes the workstation as enabling 21 CFR Part 11
compliant packing operations.

## Source Notes

The BioLink product page contains differing size ranges in different
sections: the overview lists inner diameters of 450, 600, 800, 1000 and
1200 mm; the features section lists 300, 450, 600, 800, 1000 and 1200
mm; and the detailed specification table provides data for 450, 600, 800
and 1000 mm. The ordering table includes 1200 mm models, but the
detailed specification table does not provide their dimensional and
operating data. These differences are retained here rather than
reconciled.

The source also gives a general column tube height of 700 mm, with 1000
mm customizable in the overview, while the features section separately
mentions height ranges of 550 and 570 mm. Confirm the required
configuration with BioLink before specification or procurement.
