## Product Overview

The **CytoLinX® BR** is a single-use stirred-tank bioreactor platform from BioLink, available in nominal vessel sizes from 10 L to 2,000 L. The system comprises a bioreactor vessel, controller and temperature control unit (TCU), with configurations intended for mammalian cell culture, insect cell culture and other applications requiring relatively low shear conditions.

The platform is designed to cover process development through production-scale upstream operations. Its control architecture is based on Siemens automation technology, with the 50–2,000 L models using Siemens PCS 7 / DCS architecture.

## Key Features

### Scalable single-use platform

- Standard nominal vessel sizes: 10, 50, 200, 500, 1,000 and 2,000 L.
- Designed to support process development and scale-up toward production.
- The manufacturer describes the platform as suitable for mammalian cells, insect cells and other shear-sensitive cell culture applications.

### Integrated automation and control

- The 50–2,000 L models use Siemens PCS 7-based DCS architecture.
- Automation logic is designed around the ISA-88 control standard.
- Supports real-time data acquisition and recording, online monitoring, PID control and multi-parameter cascade control.
- A single controller can be used to control multiple compatible vessels, subject to the selected configuration.

### Mixing and gas transfer

- Bottom-driven agitation design.
- The manufacturer describes a bottom impeller design intended to maintain a high linear KLa.
- Sparger configurations include micro-, medium- and macro-bubble options, with combinations available to suit different cell culture processes.

### Flexible process configuration

- Configurable pH and dissolved oxygen (DO) measurement options.
- Optional process analytical technology (PAT) tools, including pCO₂, cell density and conductivity.
- Optional gas mass flow controllers (MFCs), integrated pump units and weighing modules, depending on vessel size and configuration.
- Air-cooled or water-cooled TCU options are listed for the 50–2,000 L models.

### GMP-oriented software and documentation

- Software is stated by the manufacturer to be designed in accordance with GMP and 21 CFR Part 11 requirements.
- FAT, SAT, IQ/OQ and other validation documentation or services are available.
- OPC DA and OPC UA communication options are described for integration with third-party automation, SCADA and monitoring systems. The manufacturer states that OPC UA requires a license.

## Applications

- Mammalian cell culture
- Insect cell culture
- Upstream process development
- Seed train and production-scale cell culture
- Scale-up from process development to manufacturing
- Cell culture processes requiring low-shear operating conditions

## Control Software and System Integration

The CytoLinX® BR control system is described as providing:

- Real-time process data acquisition and recording
- PID automatic control
- Online process monitoring
- Multi-parameter cascade control
- pH, DO and temperature control
- Recipe and process control through the DCS architecture

The product page also describes OPC communication:

| Protocol | Data support | Typical integration |
|---|---|---|
| OPC DA | Real-time data | Third-party SCADA and monitoring systems |
| OPC UA | Real-time data, historical data and alarm information | SCADA, monitoring, IoT and cloud systems |

The manufacturer states that OPC DA is available without a license fee, while OPC UA requires a license. Both are stated not to require hardware configuration changes.

## Single-Use Consumables

The CytoLinX® BR platform uses dedicated single-use consumables. BioLink describes flexible sparger configurations, including micro-, medium- and macro-bubble options, to accommodate different cell culture requirements.

The product page for the broader BioLink single-use bioreactor range states that the BR bag uses RENOLIT 9101 film, with a ULDPE liquid-contact layer. Confirm material specifications, extractables and leachables documentation, sterilization method, and compatibility with the intended process for the exact consumable configuration.
